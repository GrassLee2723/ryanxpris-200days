MUSIC INTEGRATION — V6
=======================

The website uses four instrumental/piano music acts. Music starts after the user taps BEGIN OUR STORY, then continues automatically as the story is scrolled. Each act loops until the story reaches the next act. The transition is crossfaded.

Act 1 — 童话
  File: /audio/01-tong-hua.mp3
  Chapters: 01
  Role: Opening / first meeting / beginning

Act 2 — 慢慢
  File: /audio/02-man-man.mp3
  Chapters: 02–06
  Role: Mars Arc through the chapter immediately before Genting

Act 3 — Love Story
  File: /audio/03-love-story.mp3
  Chapters: 07–17
  Role: Genting through the chapter immediately before the final trip to New Zealand

Act 4 — A Thousand Years
  File: /audio/04-a-thousand-years.mp3
  Chapters: 18–21 + final
  Role: final trip to New Zealand through the ending

Music target volume is 30% so the soundtrack remains underneath the written story.

The MP3 files are not bundled. Place your four chosen piano-version MP3 files in /audio/ using the exact filenames above.
